Section five

Launch plan: phases one to three

Six phases, run in order. The first three happen in public and in stories, before anything is for sale.

Phase 1 · Build up

Engage the community and tease that something big is coming. High interaction is the only goal — no selling yet.

What to post: interactive stories on the pain points — polls on which viral recipe wasted their money, a Q&A box on the dish they keep getting wrong, and a repost of your own kitchen fail with what you learned. Reply to every box.

Phase 2 · Invitation

Announce that it is on the way and invite your audience into a private group — WhatsApp or Telegram — where all the details will be revealed first.

What to post: one feed post and a story series with the group link, plus the tease of a time-limited bonus that only exists inside the group.

Phase 3 · Anticipation

Build curiosity inside the group. Tease what is coming, show the results people can achieve, and make the timeline crystal clear so everyone is ready to act the moment doors open.

What to post: short clips from the pasta module, the exact dates doors open and close, and a daily question that keeps the group talking.

Section five, continued

Launch plan: phases four to six

This is where the $57,615 estimate is either earned or lost. Most of it happens one message at a time.

Phase 4 · Reveal

Deliver the full breakdown: what is included, the exclusive bonus, the price, and the open and close dates. Leave no doubt about what is on offer.

What to post: one long-form video in the group, then the same offer summarised in the feed for anyone outside it.

Phase 5 · Conversion

Send the enrolment link, run a poll to see who is in and who has questions, then follow up one-to-one in the DMs with everyone who raises a hand. That is where most conversions happen.

What to post: a two-option poll — "I'm in" / "I have a question" — and a personal reply to every single question, in your own voice.

Phase 6 · Urgency

Countdown messages at 24h, 12h, 6h, 2h and closed, each one making them picture exactly what they lose by staying out.

Non-negotiable: the close must be real. Reopening after a deadline destroys the credibility you need for the next launch, and your trust is the whole asset here.

Optional story follow-up

Same day, in your voice, using the phrasing your audience already answers to.

"Bafazi, quick one. How many times have you followed a viral recipe and thrown good food away?" — poll: too many / don't ask

What to watch

Count replies, not likes. A reel of yours can do 3,874 likes and mean very little; fifty people typing "I WANT" means a lot.

Treat fifty genuine replies as the green light to film the pasta module. Under that, keep testing the hook rather than building the product.
