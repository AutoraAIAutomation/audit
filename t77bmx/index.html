Section 06

Launch plan

Nothing gets built until riders have told you they want it. Four steps, in this order.

01

Ask first

Run a story poll or question box: would riders rather send their bike in, or learn to do the job themselves? Then post the test on the next page. This tests demand before anything is built.

02

Film one restoration with the series in mind

Pick a restoration you're already planning to film, chosen by your usual rule: what the bike needs and what makes the biggest difference to the rider. Film each stage fully, then send Autora the footage.

03

Waitlist, then a real launch

Invite interested riders to a waitlist and open the course to them first, with a clear launch date and a real close. Autora builds the waitlist, the sales page and the checkout.

04

Let the numbers decide

Use the replies and waitlist numbers to decide whether a second series is worth making, and when to open the first Build Crew cohort and one-to-one places.

The open question this tests: how many of the people watching your repairs can't use the workshop because of distance or your capacity. You haven't said, so this audit treats it as the thing to find out, not a fact.
