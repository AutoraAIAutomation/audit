03 · What to sell

The product ladder

Your free videos already show the method. The gap is a structured, paid version for riders who want to do the job themselves, on their own bike. Each step up sits next to the workshop rather than competing with it.

1 · The T77 Workshop Manual

Ebook · $29

What it is: your repair know-how in one written guide: diagnosing what a bike actually needs, what to replace and why, the tools, and the order of work, from headsets and wheels to grips. Autora writes it from your own videos and captions; all you do is confirm you're happy for it to go out.

Who it is for: riders who watch the repairs and want the steps on paper next to the bike.

Revenue (estimate): 292 buyers × $29 = $8,468

2 · The Workshop-at-Home Series

Online course · $200

What it is: one full restoration, from stripped frame to finished bike, filmed the way you already film, with each step broken out: diagnosis, what to replace and why, tools, and the order of work. It keeps your rule of choosing work that genuinely matters to the bike, not a repair forced for content.

Tool list inside: each lesson lists the exact tools used, linked through the tool partnerships you already have, so they become part of the product rather than a separate link in bio.

Who it is for: riders who want to do the whole job themselves but can't bring their bike to your workshop.

Revenue (estimate): 292 buyers × $200 = $58,400, before any affiliate tool income.

3 · The Build-Along Crew

Group coaching · $500

What it is: an 8-week group programme where 2 to 8 riders each work through a repair or restoration on their own bike, with a live weekly session where you diagnose problems and talk them through the next stage.

Who it is for: riders who want your eye on their bike while they do the work.

4 · Your Bike, One-to-One

One-to-one · $1,500

What it is: 8 weeks, one hour a week on video with you, planning and guiding one rider's restoration or custom build from diagnosis to the finished bike.

Who it is for: riders with a rare or special bike who would send it to your workshop if they could.

Revenue (estimate): 1 to 20 hours a week × $1,500 = $1,500 to $30,000 per 8 weeks.

06 · Order of work

Launch plan

Test first, build second. The audit infers that some of your viewers can't use the workshop because of distance or your capacity, but you haven't said how many. So the first job is to find out.

1

Ask first

Post the test carousel on the next page, plus a story poll or question box: would they rather send their bike in, or learn to do the job themselves? This tests demand before anything is built.

2

Film one restoration for the series

Pick a restoration you're already planning to film, and film it with the series in mind: each stage complete, each tool shown. Send Autora the footage.

3

Waitlist, launch date, real close

Autora sets up the waitlist for everyone who commented or voted, and opens the series to them first, with a clear launch date and a real close.

4

Let the numbers decide

Use the replies and waitlist numbers to decide whether a second series, or the first coaching cohort, is worth running.
