Launch plan

Three steps, in order

Nothing gets built before your parents tell you what they want. Each step uses what the one before it found.

1

Ask first

A story question asking parents where their teen talks most: the car, a walk or side by side.

Post it alongside the test post on page 10. The answers decide which setting the ebook and course lead with.

2

Film the course around the answers

Film one lesson per setting your parents named, in the order they ranked them, then send Autora the footage.

After the story answers and the “TALK” comments are in. Autora turns it into the course and writes the ebook alongside it.

3

Waitlist, then launch with a real close

Open a waitlist, starting with parents who applied for a free round, then launch with a clear date and a real close.

The group cohort and the paid one-to-one sit behind the same waitlist, for parents who want you with them.

What not to do

Don’t invent demand: “a few spots” is your own framing for the free rounds, not a waiting list. And no clinical claims about teens; this is coaching on conversations, in your voice.
