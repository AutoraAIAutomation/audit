Section 06

Launch plan

Three steps, in order. Nothing gets built until your audience has told you they want it.

01

Ask first

Post a story question asking leaders whether they'd join an online Elevate lab. Use the carousel on the next page as the feed version. Count the replies and comments.

02

Film the next live lab

Film your next Elevate lab with the course in mind: the teaching segments and a pair-rehearsal round. Your Lusaka lab on Saturday 3 October is the obvious candidate if a camera can be set up in time; otherwise the one after. Send Autora the footage.

03

Waitlist, then launch

Open a waitlist for everyone who said yes. Then launch with a clear date and a real close, so the first cohort fills on a deadline, not on a maybe.

Keep it honest

Don't overstate the COMESA partnership, and don't claim client results or demand that you haven't seen. The posts should rest on the method and on what happens in your labs.
