Section Four · Offer one

The Executive Resonance Protocol

$1,500 · 8 weeks · 20 spots, capped. The big bet, and the offer the monotone post has already pre-sold.

What is inside

  • A one-to-one vocal diagnostic at intake: pitch range, agility, habitual note, breath support — assessed the way a speech pathologist assesses, not the way a confidence coach guesses.
  • Live acoustic feedback sessions where they speak, you interrupt, and they hear the difference in the same minute.
  • Personalised drills from the Cadenza Method, prescribed to their diagnostic rather than handed out identically.
  • Rehearsal of their real material: the board update, the investor pitch, the press answer.
  • A closing re-test against the intake diagnostic, so the change is measured, not felt.

The promise

Your voice stops sitting on one flat note, so the room stops deciding you are uncommitted before it has heard your argument.

Who it is for

Senior executives, founders and elite sales leaders who know their delivery is leaking authority in front of boards, investors and clients — the people who watched the monotone breakdown and recognised themselves.

The first step to build it

Write the three-minute intake diagnostic script and decide the single before-and-after measure you will report to each client (pitch range used, in semitones, is the obvious one for you). Fix two cohort dates, cap the list at 20, and point cadenza.co/links at a short application form. Nothing else needs to exist before you can take a deposit.

Revenue: $15,000 at 10 spots, $30,000 at 20. Estimate.

Section Four · Offer two

The High-Stakes Turn of Phrase Masterclass

$297 · live once, then sold as a recording. This is the rhetoric offer, not the vocal one.

What is inside

  • Verbal framing: how a single word choice decides whether an answer reads as fair, evasive or commanding — the territory of your post on how one word can dismiss a nation.
  • Precision over volume: why long-winded, tangential answers sound less expert, and how to cut to the shortest credible answer under pressure.
  • Reading the room before you answer — the social intelligence layer from your Q&A post, where an identical answer lands or dies depending on the occasion.
  • Likeability and competence handled as two separate jobs, not one trade-off.
  • A hostile Q&A and media-interview drill set they can run before an appearance.

The promise

You stop losing the room in the questions, after you have already won it in the presentation.

Who it is for

Experts, spokespeople and leaders who face press, panels and board scrutiny — the audience for your pillar two content, which the audit rated high sales potential despite low reach.

The first step to build it

Run it live, once, to the first eleven people who reply to the carousel on page 11. Charge $297, record the session, and sell the recording afterwards. The live cohort pays for the build and gives you the questions that become the curriculum.

Section Four · Offer three

The 14-Day Vocal Stamina & Range Sprint

$197 · self-paced, 14 days. The rung for the person with a keynote in three weeks and no time for a cohort.

What is inside

  • Fourteen short daily drills — ten minutes each — expanding pitch agility from the habitual flat note outwards.
  • A stamina protocol for the day before and the morning of a long speaking commitment, so the voice does not fatigue halfway through.
  • A pre-stage warm-up they can run in a car park or a green room without sounding ridiculous.
  • A day-one and day-fourteen recording task, so they hear their own range change rather than taking your word for it.

The promise

Two weeks of ten-minute drills, and your voice has the range to hold a room for the length of the keynote.

Who it is for

The large group who watched the monotone reel, agreed with it, and want the physiological fix immediately without applying for anything.

The first step to build it

Record the fourteen drills in one afternoon in your usual studio setup, in the order you would prescribe them clinically. Release it only to the people who replied to the validation carousel — it is the cheapest way to find out how many of the monotone audience will actually pay, and it feeds applications up to the $1,500 cohort.

Why this rung matters. It converts the reach you already proved you can generate. The cohort converts trust; the sprint converts attention.

Section Five

The launch plan

Six phases, as set out in the audit, with what to post at each one. The whole sequence exists to move people from your feed into a place where you can speak to them directly.

01

Build up

Engage the community and tease that something is coming. Interactive stories only: polls, question boxes, DMs. Use the pain points you already know convert — does your voice sit on one note, do people check their phones when you speak, does your voice give out before the end of the keynote. High interaction is the goal, not announcements.

02

Invitation

Announce that it is on the way and invite your audience into a private group (WhatsApp or Telegram) where all the detail will be revealed first. Tease the time-limited bonus without describing it.

03

Anticipation

Build curiosity inside the group. Share what the protocol changes, the kind of before-and-after the intake diagnostic produces, and make the timeline crystal clear — dates, spot count, how the application works — so nobody is deciding logistics on the day doors open.

Reveal
