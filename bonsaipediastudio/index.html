v · Launch plan

Six phases, from first poll to closed doors

Your workshop calendar already gathers people in one place: Mysore, Vadodara, Ahmedabad on 18 October. The launch turns that attention into 20 applications.

1

Build up

Engage your community and tease that something big is coming. Interactive stories touching on their pain points and showing your results. High interaction is the goal.

Post  A poll: "What stops you making the first major cut?" A Q&A box on bending heavy branches. Before-and-after stories from the Casuarina redesign.
2

Invitation

Announce it is on the way and invite your audience into a private WhatsApp or Telegram group where all details will be revealed. Tease the time-limited exclusive bonus.

Post  The test carousel on page 9, then the group link to everyone who replied "I WANT" and to workshop attendees.
3

Anticipation

Build curiosity inside the group. Tease what is coming, share the results people can achieve, and make the timeline crystal clear so everyone is ready to act the moment doors open.

Post  Inside the group: raw material you are about to style, and the dates the apprenticeship opens and closes.

v · Launch plan, continued

Reveal, convert, close for real

4

Reveal

Deliver the full breakdown: what is included, the exclusive bonus, the price and the open and close dates. Leave no doubt about what is on offer.

Post  In the group: the eight-week outline, the live diagnostic sessions, the personal styling blueprint, $1,500, 20 places.
5

Conversion

Send the application link for one of the 20 limited spots, run a poll to see who is in or has questions, and follow up one-to-one in DMs with everyone who raises a hand. That is where most conversions happen.

Post  The application link, then a poll: "I'm in / I have a question / Not this time".
6

Urgency

Countdown messages at 24 hours, 12 hours, 6 hours, 2 hours and closed, so people see exactly what they lose by staying out. The close must be real: reopening destroys credibility for the next launch.

Post  Five short countdown messages in the group and on stories, then "closed".
Why the close matters for you: the $60,000 ceiling depends on a second cohort. A firm first close is what makes the second one believable.
