Launch plan

Ask first, then build

Three steps, in order. Nothing gets built until your audience has told you which moment they want to practise.

1

Ask first

Post the test carousel on the next page, then a story poll: which moment do you most want to practise: mornings, screens or bedtime?

Read the comments with the word "PRACTISE" and the poll results together.

2

Film the course around the top answers

One lesson per situation, starting with the moment that wins the poll. You film and send the footage; Autora builds the course and drafts the ebook from your posts for you to confirm.

3

Waitlist, then launch with a real close

Open a waitlist for parents who commented and voted. Then launch with a clear date and a real close, so parents know exactly when the doors open and when they shut.

What to keep in mind

  • Comments show interest, not sales. Treat the poll and the waitlist as the real test.
  • Keep the language practical, as you do now. No clinical claims.
  • Your safety, emotions and family stress posts already draw comments; they are natural places to mention the waitlist.
