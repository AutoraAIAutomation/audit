Test the demand before anything is built

A four-slide carousel in your voice. It names the exact pattern your posts already describe, then asks one question. The comment count tells you whether to build.

1

It isn’t laziness. Some small tasks just feel loaded.

When perfectionism, ADHD and nervous-system activation overlap, a two-minute email can feel like a threat.

3

I’m thinking of a guided programme that walks you through making the next step ridiculously small.

4

Comment “SMALL” if you’d want it.

Caption

You know the coping skills. You can explain exactly why the email feels heavy. And it still sits there. What I teach is simple: we slow it down, notice what your body is doing, and make the next step ridiculously small. The goal isn’t to do it perfectly. It’s to teach your nervous system that starting doesn’t have to feel so loaded. If you’d want me to walk you through it, comment SMALL.

Keep it clean: no clinical, diagnostic or treatment claims (this is education and coaching, not therapy), and no reader numbers or book sales.
