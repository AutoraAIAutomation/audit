Launch plan

Ask first, then build

What isn’t known yet is whether parents ask for help in the moment, between sessions. You haven’t said whether they do, so that is the first thing to test.

1

Ask first

Post a story question asking parents when they most need the pause: mornings, homework or bedtime. Alongside it, run the test post on the next page.

2

Film the course around the answers

Whichever moments parents pick become the first lessons. You film them and send the footage; we build, host and sell the course.

3

Waitlist, then a real launch

Open a waitlist, then launch with a clear date and a real close. The ebook and coaching sit either side of the course on the same sales page.

Keep it honest

Don’t claim demand or client numbers you haven’t seen, and don’t make clinical claims. Speak as a coach, as you already do.
