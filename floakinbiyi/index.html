Launch plan

Three steps, in this order. Nothing gets built until your followers have told you what they find hardest.

1

Ask first

Post a story question: "What do you find hardest about your speaking voice?" Run the four-slide test post on page 10 alongside it, and count the "VOICE" comments.

2

Film the course around the answers

Use the answers to set the lesson list, so every lesson and drill answers something your followers actually said. Film it and send the footage; Autora turns it into the course.

3

Open a waitlist, then launch

Open a waitlist for everyone who commented or replied. Then launch with a clear date and a real close, so the decision has a deadline.

Why this order: the audit's open question is whether founders want structured voice work rather than a one-off session. Step 1 answers it with your own audience before anything is built.
