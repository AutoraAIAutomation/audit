Section Five · The launch structure

How we'd launch
the system.

Six phases. Each one builds the belief needed for the next. The close at the end must be real; reopening destroys credibility for the next launch.

1Phase

Build up

Engage the community and signal that something is coming. Interactive stories, polls and Q&A boxes on the smells, pests and confusion that stop people starting, alongside your own soil results.

2Phase

Invitation

Announce it and invite the audience into a private WhatsApp or Telegram group where the details will be shared first. Mention a time-limited bonus for that group.

3Phase

Anticipation

Build curiosity inside the group. Share the transformation people can expect and make the timeline crystal clear so everyone is ready the moment doors open.

4Phase

Reveal

The full breakdown: what's included, the bonus, the price and the open/close dates. Leave no doubt about what's on the table.

5Phase

Conversion

Send the enrolment link, run a poll to see who's in or has questions, and follow up one-to-one in the DMs with everyone who raises a hand. This is where most sales happen.

6Phase

Urgency

Countdown messages at 24h, 12h, 6h, 2h and closed. Make them picture another year of scraps going in the bin. Then close for real.

Why this order

Nothing is sold until the audience has raised a hand three times: in stories, in the group and in the DMs. By the time the link goes out, the people buying have already decided.
