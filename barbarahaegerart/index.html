Section five

The launch plan

Six phases. This is the structure that gets the 1,663 figure anywhere near real, because it reaches people over weeks rather than in one post.

1

Build up

Engage the community and tease that something is coming. Interactive stories — polls, question boxes, DMs — on the pain points, plus your own results. High interaction is the only goal here.

2

Invitation

Announce that it is on the way and invite your audience into a private group (WhatsApp or Telegram) where all the details will be revealed. Tease a time-limited exclusive bonus.

3

Anticipation

Build curiosity inside the group. Tease what is coming, share the results people can achieve, and make the timeline crystal clear so everyone is ready to act the moment doors open.

4

Reveal

The full breakdown: what is included, the exclusive bonus, the price, and the open and close dates. Leave no doubt about what is on offer.

5

Conversion

Send the enrolment link, run a poll to see who is in and who has questions, then follow up one-to-one in the DMs with everyone who raises a hand. That is where most of the 166 come from.

6

Urgency

Countdown messages at 24h, 12h, 6h, 2h and closed, each one making them picture what staying out costs them. The close must be real — reopening destroys credibility for the next launch.

Where the ebook and the playbook sit

Sell the $29 ebook before Phase 1 of the big launch ever starts, and give it a short version of the same sequence: a week of stories, one clear reveal post, then DMs to everyone who replied. That push is exactly what its 166–249 buyers assume. Each playbook edition gets the same treatment on a one-week cycle.
