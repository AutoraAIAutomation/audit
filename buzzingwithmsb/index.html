Launch plan

Ask first, then build

1

Ask first

Post a story question asking coaches which teacher they're stuck with right now, and why. Their answers tell you which cases the course and the coaching should start with.

2

Film the course from the most common answers

Pick the teacher situations that come up most and film Matching the Strategy to the Teacher around them. You send the footage; Autora builds the rest.

3

Waitlist, readers first, then a real launch

Open a waitlist and invite readers of Expand Your Coaching Toolbox first. Then launch with a clear date and a real close.

"We really have to work hard to understand teacher motivations, because the way they interact with us can give us clues about what they need from us and how to proceed next!"You, in a DM on 30/09. Slide 2 of your first post is built on this.

Two things to avoid

  • Don't imply the book, the Start-Up course or the toolbox falls short. The new products are the next step after them.
  • Don't invent demand or sales. Let the comments and the waitlist show what is there.
