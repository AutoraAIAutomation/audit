Launch plan

Four steps, in this order

Ask before you build, and measure what happens after. Nothing here needs a new platform or a bigger following.

1

Ask first

Post a story question box: “Where do you get stuck most when you don’t know what to say?” Their answers shape the lessons, in their own words. Alongside it, post the test carousel on the next page.

2

Build around the one framework

Write the programme around the framework behind your reel, using the client situations you already see most often, matched against what the question box surfaced.

3

Open a waitlist, then launch to it

Everyone who commented “FRAMEWORK” gets the waitlist link. Launch to that list with a clear date and a real close, so there is a reason to decide.

4

Track who books

Count how many buyers go on to book a consultation. That number tells you whether a second framework (voice and delivery, say, where your biggest reels sit) is worth packaging next.

What to post

Keep your normal technique reels going throughout. Each one on thinking-on-your-feet is a natural place to say “the full framework is coming, join the waitlist”.
