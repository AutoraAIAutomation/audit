Where the number comes from · averages

Three ways to read the middle

For the averages, each coaching product is counted at the middle of its 8-week range, not its year figure: group coaching (1,000 + 4,000) ÷ 2 = $2,500; one-to-one (1,500 + 30,000) ÷ 2 = $15,750. In order, the four figures are $2,500, $8,033, $15,750 and $55,400.

Median · the mean of the two middle figures
($8,033 + $15,750) ÷ 2 = $23,783 ÷ 2 = $11,891.50 Estimate
Mean · the four figures divided by 4
($2,500 + $8,033 + $15,750 + $55,400) ÷ 4 = $81,683 ÷ 4 = $20,420.75 Estimate
Mid-range · (minimum + maximum) ÷ 2, using the year maximum
($8,033 + $180,000) ÷ 2 = $188,033 ÷ 2 = $94,016.50 Estimate

Launch plan

Test, ask, build, launch

  1. Post the test below first. Before anything is built. The number of "FRAMEWORK" comments is the demand test.
  2. Ask in your stories. A question box: where do you get stuck most when you don't know what to say? Their answers shape the lessons.
  3. Build around the one framework. Use the client situations you already see most often. Autora writes the ebook; you film the course lessons.
  4. Open a waitlist, then launch to it with a clear date and a real close. Track how many buyers go on to book a consultation.
