Conversion assumptions

Section four

The launch plan · phases one to three

Six phases. Nothing is built until Phase 2 has proven people will raise their hand. The whole sequence runs on stories and a private group, which is where your audience already talks to you.

01

Build up

Engage your community and tease that something big is coming. Interactive stories — polls, question boxes, DMs — touching directly on their pain points, plus proof of your own results. High interaction is the only goal of this phase. No product, no price, no link.

02

Invitation

Announce it is on the way and invite your audience into a private group on WhatsApp or Telegram where all the details will be revealed. Tease the time-limited exclusive bonus. The size of that group is your real forecast — compare it against the 637–955 buyers in the model.

03

Anticipation

Build curiosity inside the group. Tease what is coming, share the results people can achieve, and make the timeline crystal clear so everyone is ready to act the moment doors open. Daily presence, short messages, answer everything.

Phases 1 to 3 cost you nothing but attention. If the group does not fill, you have lost a week, not a product.

Section four, continued

The launch plan · phases four to six

04

Reveal

Deliver the full breakdown inside the group: what is included, the exclusive bonus, the price, and the open and close dates. Leave no doubt about what is on offer and no question unanswered.

05

Conversion

Send the enrolment link. Run a poll to see who is in and who has questions, then follow up one-to-one in the DMs with everyone who raises a hand. That is where most of your conversions happen — not on the sales page.

06

Urgency

Countdown messages at 24h, 12h, 6h, 2h and closed, each one making them picture exactly what they lose by staying out. The close must be real. Reopening destroys your credibility for the next launch, and there will be a next launch.

The first post · validate before you build

Post this carousel before anything is built. Black background, white text, your voice. Three slides.

Slide 1 — the hook

Making fast money means nothing if you have to sacrifice your dignity and your sanity just to pay rent.

Slide 2 — the agitation

Most people in this game never learn how to sell with their mind. They run themselves into the ground, let people cross their boundaries, and end up with zero leverage. Working hard isn’t the problem. The lack of an actual system is.

Slide 3 — curiosity and call to action

I put together the exact psychological blueprint to command high value, set bulletproof boundaries, and keep your cash. Reply ‘I WANT’ if this resonates with you.

What to measure

Count the ‘I WANT’ replies in the first 48 hours. Every reply is a name for the Phase 2 group. Reply to each one personally and ask the single question: what is the part of this you most need fixed?

Then build

Build the module their answers repeat most, first. That way the product is written by the buyers before the first dollar is asked for, and the launch copy is already on your phone.
