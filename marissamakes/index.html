Launch plan

Ask first, then build

Three steps, in this order. Nothing is built until your followers have told you what they need.

1
First · stories

Ask the question

Post a story question: which ingredient do you never know what to pair with? Run the test post on the next page alongside it. Fall produce is already on your mind and theirs: apples, pumpkin, grapes, honeynut squash.

2
Next · your kitchen

Film the course around the answers

Take the ingredients people name most and build the Cook by Season lessons around them. You film; Autora turns the footage into the course. The herb-pairing guide is written from your posts at the same time.

3
Then · feed and stories

Waitlist, then a dated launch

Open a waitlist, then launch with a clear date and a real close. Everyone who commented "HERBS" or answered the story question hears first.

Why this order

The demand for cooking without a recipe is not yet confirmed. The question and the test post confirm it, and tell you which dishes to teach, before you spend a day filming.
