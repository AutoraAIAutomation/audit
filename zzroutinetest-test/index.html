Launch plan

Test first, film second.

Three steps, in this order. Nothing gets built until your followers have told you, with their email address, that they want it.

1
Weeks 1–2

Post the shaping clip and open a waitlist

A 60-second clip of the shaping step, with a caption asking who wants the full class online and a link to a waitlist form. The script is on the next page.

Target: 100 sign-ups in two weeks.

2
Once the waitlist reaches 100

Film the class properly

You’ve been meaning to. Film one class in four parts: starter, mixing, shaping, baking. Open the course to the waitlist first at £49.

3
After the first buyers

Offer the membership

Invite course buyers to the £15 a month membership and run the first live Q&A and bake-along.

If the waitlist falls short of 100, that tells you something before you’ve spent a weekend filming. Ask the people who did sign up what they’d need from it, then decide.
