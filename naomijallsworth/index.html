The two coaching ranges

Group coaching

Per 8-week cohort: 2 × $500 = $1,000 up to 8 × $500 = $4,000. A year holds six cohorts, so up to 48 places (8 × 6), which would be 48 × $500 = $24,000. With 38 buyers, the group programme could make you 38 × $500 = $19,000 a year.

One-to-one coaching

Per 8 weeks: 1 hour a week (1 client) × $1,500 = $1,500 up to 20 hours a week (20 clients) × $1,500 = $30,000. Six 8-week blocks at 20 hours a week hold 120 programmes (20 × 6), or $180,000. With 38 buyers, one-to-one could make you 38 × $1,500 = $57,000 a year.

Launch plan

Ask, film, then open the doors

Three steps, in order. Nothing gets built until your followers have answered the first one.

1
First · a story question

Ask first

Ask followers who missed a weekend place, or live too far away, whether they'd start learning online. The first post (next page) does this, and the replies tell us how many people that really is.

2
Next · at a session you already run

Film the course skills

Shelter, fire, navigation and foraging, filmed during a session already in your diary, so the course costs you no extra days. Send the footage; Autora does the rest.

3
Then · waitlist and launch

Open a waitlist, then launch

Start with the people who ask about the weekend places, then launch with a clear date and a real close.

Two things not to do

Don't claim an online course replaces hands-on practice outdoors. Frame it as preparation and practice.

Don't invent how many people missed a place. Let the replies to the first post give the number.

Comment "WILD" if you'd want it.

Why this one

It speaks to the people the weekends can't fit, in the same words you use about your teaching, and it asks one plain question, the way your friction fire post did. Every "WILD" is a name for the waitlist.

It promises preparation, not a replacement for a weekend, and makes no claim about how many people missed out.
