Germany Expat Wealth Accelerator

“I make decent money in Germany, but after taxes and living costs I have no system to grow it.”

That sentence is the root problem underneath your best-performing content. This offer answers it end to end.

What it is

An 8-week intensive group mentorship with direct access to you, capped at 20 people, at €1,500 a seat.

Who it is for

International professionals working in Germany who earn a good gross salary, feel lost in the system, and have no structured investment plan.

What is inside

  • Tax class optimisation — the pay slip walkthrough from your €60,000 breakdown, applied to each member's own numbers
  • Local investing setup — getting invested properly from inside Germany, not from advice written for another country
  • Pension planning — what the German pension will actually pay them, and what has to sit on top of it
  • A custom emergency fund blueprint — your own €20,000-in-12-months run, turned into a plan built around their salary, without pausing investments

The promise

In eight weeks they leave with their tax position corrected, money invested through German accounts, a pension gap they can name, and a dated emergency fund plan. One roadmap for money in Germany, instead of forty saved reels.

First step to build it

Write the eight week titles on one page before anything else. If you cannot name week five, the programme is not ready to sell. Then run the carousel on page 11 and open a waiting list.

The other two rungs

Offer two · Net Salary & Tax Optimisation Clinic — €149

What it is. A live, focused workshop plus an audit system where members open their own German pay slip, work through tax deductions, and find the unnecessary insurance and subscription drains.

The difference. It fixes cash leaks and maximises monthly take-home pay. It does not touch long-term investing, which is what the Accelerator is for.

Who it is for. The people commenting on your tax class and “where your tax money goes” videos who are not ready for €1,500 but are very ready to stop overpaying.

The promise. They leave the session with a specific euro figure they are going to recover each month, and the steps to claim it.

Revenue estimate: 10–15 buyers, 2%–3% of the engaged 500 = €1,490–€2,235. Its real job is to create your first buyers, who are the warmest possible audience for the Accelerator.

Section five

The launch, phases one to three

Six phases. The first three build the room; nothing is sold yet.

01

Build up

Engage your community and tease that something big is coming. Interactive stories — polls, question boxes, DMs — on the pain points your comments already name: the tax hit, the balance that will not move, the missing plan. High interaction is the goal, not clicks. Keep posting your emergency fund day count; it is your proof.

02

Invitation

Announce it is on the way and invite your audience into a private group — WhatsApp or Telegram — where all the details will be revealed first. Tease the time-limited exclusive bonus. Every DM reply from the carousel on page 11 gets a personal invite to that group.

03

Anticipation

Build curiosity inside the group. Tease what is coming, share the results people can achieve, and make the timeline crystal clear, so everyone is ready to act the moment doors open. This is where you answer the tax class and pay slip questions in front of everybody, not one by one.

Why the private group matters to the number

The conservative case needs 8 buyers. Finding 8 people at €1,500 from a feed post is hard; finding them from a self-selected group of people who raised their hand is normal. The group is the difference between the €12,000 estimate and no launch at all.

Section five, continued

The launch, phases four to six

The last three phases are where the 20 seats are sold — or are not.

04

Reveal

Deliver the full breakdown inside the group: what is included week by week, the exclusive bonus, the price, and the open and close dates. Leave no doubt about what is on offer. State the 20-seat cap plainly and explain why it exists — direct access to you does not scale past it.

05

Conversion

Send the application link for one of the limited spots. Run a poll to see who is in and who has questions, then follow up one-to-one in the DMs with everyone who raised a hand. That is where most of the conversions happen — expect the people paying €1,500 to want a short conversation first.

06

Urgency

Countdown messages at 24h, 12h, 6h, 2h and closed, each one making them picture exactly what they lose by staying out: another year of the same pay slip. The close must be real. Reopening destroys your credibility for the next launch, and the next launch is where the ladder pays.

Content during launch

Salary breakdowns and budgeting systems only. Those two pillars carry your buying intent. Save the UPI and city ranking formats for after the cart closes.

Keep the disclaimer

You already run an educational disclaimer on tax content. Keep the same wording on every sales asset — it costs nothing and protects the whole ladder.

The first post

Post this before you build anything. Black background, white text, three slides, in your own explainer voice. It tests whether the demand is real at no cost.

Slide 1 · the hook

You earn €60k+ in Germany.

So why does your bank balance barely move each month?

Slide 2 · the agitation

It is not that you do not earn enough.

It is the 40% tax hit, sneaky deductions, and no clear system for your money.

Working hard in a foreign country without a local wealth plan is exhausting.

Slide 3 · the call to action

I built a private system to optimise your take-home pay, stop the leaks, and invest properly in Germany.

Reply ‘I WANT’ if this resonates with you.

How to read the result

You need 8 buyers for the conservative case. As a working guide, aim for 40 or more ‘I WANT’ replies before you open the cart — that gives you a DM list deep enough to convert 8 at €1,500. Fewer than 20 means run the Clinic at €149 first and come back to the Accelerator.

What to do with every reply

Answer each one, ask which part hurts most — the tax, the budget or the investing — and invite them straight into the private group from phase two. Their words become your sales page.
