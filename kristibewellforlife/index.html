Launch plan

Ask first, then build

Three steps, in order. Nothing gets built until your bakers have told you what goes wrong with their starters.

1

Ask first

Post a story question asking bakers what goes wrong with their starter. Run the four-slide test post on page 11 alongside it. Count who answers and save what they say: their words become the course outline and the ebook's chapter titles.

2

Film the course around the answers

Plan your filming around the problems bakers named, then film your bakes in your kitchen and micro bakery and send us the footage. Autora writes the ebook from your posts at the same time and sends it to you to confirm.

3

Open a waitlist, then launch

Open a waitlist for the people who replied, then launch with a clear date and a real close. Autora builds the pages, checkout and emails; you post about it the way you already post about the Double-Feed Method.

What not to do

Don't treat comment counts as proven demand, and don't promise sales or results. The test exists to find out what your bakers actually want before you film a single lesson.
